# Experiment: Humor Processing

## Hypothesis

Humorous stimuli will produce distinct activation patterns in brain regions
associated with humor comprehension: the temporal-parietal junction (TPJ),
medial prefrontal cortex (mPFC), ventral striatum (reward), and
superior temporal sulcus (STS) — areas implicated in mentalizing,
incongruity detection, and reward processing.

## Protocol

- **Stimuli:** Short video clips — funny vs. neutral matched for content
- **Design:** Event-related (clips presented with jittered ISI)
- **Conditions:**
  - `funny`: humorous video clips
  - `neutral`: non-humorous matched control clips
- **Contrasts:**
  - `funny - neutral`: humor-specific activation
  - `funny`: overall response to humorous stimuli
- **Analysis:** Whole-brain GLM contrast maps + ROI extraction

## Expected Results

Based on Vrticka et al. (2013), Mobbs et al. (2003), and others:
- TPJ (PGi): incongruity resolution / mentalizing
- mPFC: humor appreciation / reward prediction
- STS (STSv): social/communicative processing
- Ventral striatum: reward response to humor
